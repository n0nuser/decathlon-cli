## `decathlon product`

Read a product page: models, sizes, prices and stock

Aliases: `item`

### `decathlon product open`

Open the product page in the browser

```bash
decathlon product open PRODUCT
```

### `decathlon product show`

Show every colour and size of a product, with online and store availability

```bash
decathlon product show PRODUCT
```

Show a product. PRODUCT is a product URL, or GROUP/MODEL as printed in the
ID column of 'search' (the product group id and one colour's model id).

```bash
decathlon product show 303295/8513471 --format pretty
decathlon product show https://www.decathlon.es/es/p/tienda-de-campana-2-personas-quechua-mh100/303295/c251c98c74m8513471
```
